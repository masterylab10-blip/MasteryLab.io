Dominican Bachata Director

ÁNGELA MARIANO

Dominican Bachata Specialist | Ladies Styler

Born in Puglia, Italy, Ángela began dancing at the age of 5. Her extensive training spans ballroom & Latin, contemporary, street, hip-hop, lyrical jazz, modern, Afro-Cuban salsa, Dominican Bachata, and Russian ballet.

She is a renowned ladies style specialist whose Dominican Bachata curriculum focuses on authentic rhythm, hip movements, cultural roots, and connection.

XIDAN

Bachata Sensual Specialist | Ladies Styler
