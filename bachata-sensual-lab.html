THE INSTRUCTORS

CHRISTINA & REBECA

Discover their Art

Christina & Rebeca are international Bachata Artists, multiple World Finalists, and certified Bachata Sensual Instructors & Examiners of Future Teachers.

Both have taught internationally as Leader & Follower while being solo Artists as well as with various partners before becoming Dance Partners and regularly teach alongside Korke the creator of Bachata Sensual.

Christina is a Swiss Champion (2019) before having moved to Barcelone to dedicate herself to Dance Fulltime. She brings deep expertise in Role Switching methodology, exploring versatility, adaptability, and equality within partner dance.

Rebeca is the creator of the Body Movement methodology, focused on body awareness, biomechanics, and expressive movement applied to Bachata Sensual.

Together, they offer two complementary, high-level methodologies that merge technique, body intelligence, and artistic expression, redefining Bachata Sensual from a refined and innovative perspective.

Two Programs, One Vision

🎯 Dancers Lab Led by Ale & Xidan

A one-time immersive lab experience for social dancers and performers. Date coming soon!

🎓 Teachers Education Led by Christina & Rebeca

A structured 3-level certification program for current and aspiring instructors.

All participants must complete all three levels to finish the full program and receive certification.

  • If you start with Perfect Start → you continue with Almost There, then I Made It
  • If you start with Almost There → you must also complete Perfect Start and I Made It
  • If you start with I Made It → you must also complete Perfect Start and Almost There

Each level must be completed to receive the full program certification.
