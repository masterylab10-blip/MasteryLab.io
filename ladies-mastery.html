BEYOND TYPICAL WORKSHOPS

The lab is educational and focused to give you the roots of dominican bachata and the magic of bachata sensual.

MEET THE ARTISTS

Ángela Mariano

Dominican Bachata Specialist · Ladies Styler (5 Hours)

Discover her Art

Born in Puglia, Italy, Ángela began dancing at the age of 5. Her training spans ballroom & Latin, contemporary, street, hip-hop, lyrical jazz, modern, Afro-Cuban salsa, bachata, LA style salsa, salsa on2, and Russian ballet. She has appeared on multiple Italian TV shows and competed in prestigious dance contests since childhood.

Renowned ladies styler specialist. Her Dominican Bachata class focuses on:

  • ✓ Hip movements & authentic Dominican styling
  • ✓ Individual footwork combined into lady styling
  • ✓ Connection, rhythm & cultural roots of bachata
  • ✓ Basic fundamentals — solo & with a partner
  • ✓ Open level · No partner required

Xidan

Bachata Sensual Specialist · Ladies Styler (5 Hours)

Discover her Art

Xidan has shared her passion in Bachata for 12 years. Her teaching focuses on elegant body movement, clean technique, musicality, styling, and confidence — helping dancers make their movement feel as good as it looks.

With a smooth and modern style inspired by Bachata Sensual and other dance influences, Xidan guides students to move with more fluidity, control, and authentic expression.

Xidan's Workshop

Lady Styling & Footwork

Join Xidan for a Bachata lady styling / footwork workshop designed to help you move with clarity, control and expression. Confidence comes when the body understands what it is doing — come ready to unlock your expression and bring more of yourself into every movement.
