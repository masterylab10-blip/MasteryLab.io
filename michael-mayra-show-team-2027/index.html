MEET THE ARTISTS

Michael & Mayra are top world-recognized international Bachata artists, choreographers, and newlyweds from Lebanon and Argentina. Sub-champions of the South American Bachata Open, they are known for blending high technical precision with elegance, emotion, and artistic expression.

Having traveled to over 60 countries, they share their unique methodology, Dance Healing — a holistic approach combining dance, psychology, and coaching to inspire transformation worldwide.

Discover their Art

MICHAEL

Discover their Art

Michael has been a Latin and Ballroom dancer since he was a teenager, and has been Lebanese Latin and Ballroom Dancing Champion.

He has always been a highly technical dancer, and this has helped his teaching, something he has been doing since he was only 16 years old.

With his love for teaching and a lifetime of experience, he can help students transform and rapidly achieve their goals.

MAYRA

Discover their Art

Mayra became familiar with a wide variety of styles: from classical ballet to bachata, from aerial acrobatics to hip-hop, from flamenco to contemporary, and from jazz to ice skating. This latter has been a big influence on their dance, since spins are incorporated in most of Michael and Mayra's routines.

As well as a dancer, Mayra also worked as a model and actress in her native Argentina.
