International Artist

NATALIA LOPEZ

Performance Artist | Choreographer | Coach

Based in Zurich, Switzerland, Natalia has been conquering audiences around the world since 2009. She is a multi-talented stage performer, dancer, singer, and international instructor.

"Nothing compares to the magical thrill of going on stage and taking people’s breath away, and that’s exactly what I love to do each time I perform."

— Natalia Lopez

Her artistic career has been created through immense passion, dedication, and self-awareness. Natalia brings world-class experience to her teachings and performances, inspiring the next generation of dancers.
